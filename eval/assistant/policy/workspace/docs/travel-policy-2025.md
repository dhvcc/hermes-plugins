# Travel Policy (version 2)

Status: approved by the board on 2025-02-12, in force from 2025-03-01.

## Flights
- Economy class is reimbursed in full.
- Business class is allowed only when the scheduled flight time of each leg is over 6 hours.
  Reimbursement for a business class round trip is capped at 2,500 EUR. Anything above the cap is paid by the employee.

## Hotels
- Reimbursed up to the nightly cap for the destination city listed in `city-rates.md`.
  The part of the nightly rate above the cap is not reimbursed.

## Per diem
- 60 EUR for each full day abroad.
- Departure and return days count as travel days and get 50% of the per diem.
